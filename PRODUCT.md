# Product

## Register

Product

## Users

HitKeep serves privacy-conscious product, engineering, and marketing teams that need useful product and conversion analytics, plus operators who want a low-maintenance self-hosted deployment.

## Product Purpose

HitKeep turns cookieless event data into clear, actionable analytics while keeping deployment, privacy controls, and day-to-day operation straightforward.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, pragmatic, and quietly confident. The product should explain security and operational boundaries directly without sounding alarming or promotional.

## Anti-references

- Opaque ad-tech dashboards that conceal data collection or security boundaries.
- Dependency-heavy enterprise administration interfaces that make routine operations feel complex.
- Decorative SaaS surfaces that trade information hierarchy and legibility for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

1. Make privacy, permissions, and one-time security boundaries visible at the point of action.
2. Prefer explicit operator controls and plain-language feedback over clever hidden behavior.
3. Reuse established interaction patterns so equivalent actions behave and look equivalent.
4. Keep high-value information easy to scan without overloading the interface.
5. Preserve the same understandable product foundation across managed and self-hosted deployments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a WCAG AA baseline: keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, semantic status announcements, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and no reliance on color alone. Keep all user-facing dashboard copy available in the seven supported locales (`en`, `de`, `es`, `fr`, `it`, `nl`, and `pt`).
