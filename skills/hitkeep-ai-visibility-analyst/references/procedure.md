# AI visibility procedure

1. Clarify whether the question concerns fetch access, downstream referred visits, path correlation, failures, content opportunity, or organic-search context.
2. Analyze AI visibility with only supported dimensions. Add site overview and imported Search Console context when they materially explain the result.
3. Separate crawler fetches from human visits. A fetched path with later AI-referred visits is directional correlation, not proof that the same assistant produced the visit.
4. Separate assistant families from individual names, treat citation yield directionally at small samples, and inspect status, path, and resource patterns before blaming content quality for failures.
5. Never claim black-box ranking, citation, answer placement, or source-specific path attribution that the evidence does not prove.

Report the finding, fetch-versus-referral evidence, relevant traffic or search context, the next content, access, or tracking action, and sample, import, or mapping caveats.
