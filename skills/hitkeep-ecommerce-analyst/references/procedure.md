# Ecommerce and conversion procedure

1. Establish the current and comparison periods, business outcome, and overall traffic context.
2. Decompose revenue movement into traffic volume, conversion rate, average order value, product mix, and source or campaign mix.
3. Add event, funnel, goal, and Web Vitals context only when checkout, forms, navigation, or page performance may explain movement.
4. Treat city, provider, and ASN rows as aggregate geographic or infrastructure context, never identity.
5. Do not request customer-level orders or raw hits through aggregate analytics tools; use supported exports or takeout when authorized.

Report the revenue or conversion verdict, baseline comparison, source or product evidence, supporting traffic, event, funnel, or performance context, actions, and attribution or sample caveats.
